# Changelog

## 0.2.0 — The Front Door (2026-11-03)
- A real home page with a spinning pixel Earth, download links and everything you need to know.
- New main menu with a cinematic tour of the world and its campuses.
- Up to five companies in save slots, with save codes and file export/import.
- New company setup: difficulty presets, founder perks and custom starts with a shareable seed.
- Bit now walks new players through the first ten minutes, and the Field Guide explains the real science behind the game.
- A full Settings screen: gameplay, display (CRT filter, battery saver, UI size), audio volumes and accessibility options.
- Music: a chiptune theme on the menu and quiet ambience in game.
- Install it: play offline as an app on phones and computers, or download the desktop version.

## 0.1.0 — First Light (2026-11-03)
- Act I, The Garage: from a rented rack to a Series A.
- A real-Earth pixel world map with live day/night, 12 regions and 36 build sites.
- Power, chips, shipping through real chokepoints, training, research and safety.
